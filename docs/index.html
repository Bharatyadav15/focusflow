FocusFlow

A Pomodoro timer, project planner and daily timeline for Windows, connected to Google Calendar and Google Tasks.

What it does

  • Pomodoro timer with an always-on-top floating widget and a taskbar widget.
  • Google Calendar: view today's events, start a focus session from any event, and time-block tasks onto your calendar.
  • Google Tasks: view, create and complete your tasks without leaving the app.
  • Projects: local project manager with milestones, priorities and estimated Pomodoros.
  • Daily timeline: see where your day went and log focus blocks to your calendar.
  • AI assistant control (optional): a local, password-protected server lets tools such as Hermes, Claude or Gemini manage your tasks and timer through MCP.

Your data

FocusFlow is a local-first desktop app. Your projects, tasks and settings are stored on your own PC. The app talks only to Google's APIs, and only to read and write your own Calendar and Tasks. There is no FocusFlow server, no analytics and no advertising. Read the full Privacy Policy.

Google permissions requested

  • https://www.googleapis.com/auth/calendar: read and create calendar events for time-blocking and focus logging.
  • https://www.googleapis.com/auth/tasks: read and update your Google Tasks.
