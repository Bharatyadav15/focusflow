FocusFlow Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 15, 2026

FocusFlow ("the app") is a Windows desktop application developed by Bharat Yadav. This policy explains what data the app accesses and how it is handled. Back to homepage.

Summary

  • All your data stays on your own computer.
  • The app has no server of its own. The developer cannot see your data.
  • The app communicates only with Google's services, to read and write your own Google Calendar and Google Tasks.
  • Your data is never sold, shared with third parties, used for advertising or used to train AI models.

Data the app accesses

If you choose to sign in with Google, the app requests these permissions:

  • Google Calendar (auth/calendar): to show your events, create events when you time-block a task or log a focus session, and update or delete events you change inside the app.
  • Google Tasks (auth/tasks): to show, create, update and complete your tasks.

The app does not request your email address, contacts, files, or any other Google data. You can use the app without signing in to Google; calendar and task sync is then simply unavailable.

How data is stored

  • Projects, tasks, calendar and task caches, timer sessions and settings are stored in a file on your PC at %APPDATA%\focusflow.
  • Your Google sign-in token and OAuth client secret are stored in the Windows Credential Manager.
  • Nothing is uploaded to the developer or to any third party.

How data is used and shared

  • Google data is used only to provide the features you see in the app (timer linked to events, task and calendar views, time-blocking and logging).
  • It is not transferred to anyone else, not used for advertising, and not used to train generative AI or machine-learning models.
  • No humans, including the developer, read your data.

Optional AI-assistant integration

The app can run a local server on your own machine (127.0.0.1, protected by a secret token) so AI tools that you set up yourself can manage your tasks and timer through MCP. If you connect such a tool, that tool receives the information it asks for and is governed by its own provider's privacy policy. The app itself does not send your data to any AI service.

Google API Services User Data Policy

FocusFlow's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Retention and deletion

  • Signing out inside the app removes the stored Google token from your PC.
  • You can revoke the app's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  • To delete all local data, uninstall the app and delete the folder %APPDATA%\focusflow. You can also remove the entries named google_tokens.FocusFlow and google_client_secret.FocusFlow in Windows Credential Manager.
  • Events and tasks stored in your Google account remain there, under Google's policies, until you delete them.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect any data from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
